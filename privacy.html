Privacy, plainly

Your pages stay in your browser.

Last updated 19 October 2026 · Price Auto Converter by Harry Rama

Price conversion

The extension reads supported price text and currency signals on pages where it runs, then changes the displayed text locally. It does not send page contents, URLs, browsing history, cookies, form values or passwords to the developer.

Exchange-rate services

The extension requests USD-based exchange-rate data from open.er-api.com and jsDelivr’s currency-api package. Those providers receive normal network information, such as your IP address. Requests omit cookies and referrers. The requested rate URL does not include the website you are visiting or your chosen currency.

Preferences and local storage

Currency choices, appearance preferences, fee buffers and website rules use Chrome storage. Chrome may sync preferences and site rules if browser sync is enabled. Cached exchange rates and a rolling list of up to 40 extension error codes stay in local extension storage. Error entries contain only a timestamp and a fixed code, not page content or stack traces. You can clear that history from the report page, and removing the extension removes its local data.

Reports you choose to share

Reports are prepared locally and are not uploaded automatically or synced by the extension. They contain version, relevant settings, rate freshness, counts, safe element shapes and recent error codes. Hostnames and original price examples are off by default and can be included after you select them. Review the preview before copying, downloading or sharing.

Opening GitHub does not send the report. The JSON is copied locally so you can paste it. If you submit a GitHub issue, its contents are public and subject to GitHub’s privacy policy. Reports are used to diagnose and fix the extension. You can edit or remove information you posted on GitHub or ask the maintainer for help removing it.

No advertising or data sales

The extension has no analytics, advertising trackers, automatic bug-report uploads or data sales. It uses information only to provide and improve price conversion and user-requested support, consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy’s Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Contact the maintainer through the project’s GitHub issues. Do not post private purchase, account or financial details in a public issue.
